## Context

This plan fixes the remaining high-impact pages: NewLaboratoryOrder (multi-step lab order form) has 6 instances of `grid-cols-2` plus a parent flex container that needs responsive stacking. Finance forms (NewExpense, NewPayroll, NewCashTransfer, PayrollCalculate) have multi-column desktop layouts with `md:grid-cols-2` or `lg:grid-cols-2` but may still overflow on smaller tablets. NewPurchase and EditPurchase have `grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-2` parent wrappers but inner grids still need responsive fixes (handled in 15-02), plus any remaining padding issues.

All UI text is in Spanish. Components stay under 200 lines.
